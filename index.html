01 / About Me

Engineering mindset. Software ambition. Data-driven curiosity.

I’m building my career around the intersection of computer science, software development and data science.

I’m Sreerang P, an undergraduate student pursuing Electrical & Computer Engineering at TKM College of Engineering while also pursuing a BS in Data Science from IIT Madras. Although my engineering background gives me exposure to hardware and engineering systems, my strongest professional interest is on the computing and software side — programming, algorithms, data, AI and building useful digital products.

Who I am

I’m a curious engineering student who enjoys understanding how things work and then trying to build them. I like mathematics, logical problem solving, programming and experimenting with new technologies. I learn particularly well when theory is followed by implementation.

Where I'm heading

I’m working toward software and data-oriented opportunities where I can combine programming, DSA, mathematical thinking and data science. My long-term direction is toward strong technical work in software, data science and AI.

How I learn

My preferred loop is simple: learn a concept, build something with it, encounter problems, debug, understand the underlying reason, and improve the implementation. Projects are a major part of how I turn learning into practical skill.

What I enjoy

I enjoy programming, mathematics, DSA, web development, data science, AI, computer vision and productivity tools. I’m especially interested in projects that turn a real-world problem into a clean technical solution.
